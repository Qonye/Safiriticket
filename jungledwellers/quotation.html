A. When Acting as a DMC for Travel Agents

1. Relationship

  • Jungle Dwellers provides ground handling, tours, transport, and related services to the contracting travel agent ("the Agent").
  • The Agent is responsible for its own clients, including travel insurance, travel documents, and communication of these terms.

2. Payments and Cancellations

  • A non-refundable deposit (as specified in the quotation) is required to confirm a booking.
  • The balance is payable no later than 6 weeks before travel, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Payments must be made in the currency and bank account shown on the invoice. Late payments may attract 5% monthly interest or cancellation.

Cancellations by the Agent:

  • More than 45 days before departure: deposit forfeited.
  • 45-30 days before departure: 50% fee.
  • Less than 30 days before departure: 100% fee.

Cancellation by the Company (Force Majeure):

  • Refund of monies received or alternative arrangement offered. No further liability to the Agent or their clients.

3. Liability

  • Jungle Dwellers acts as a ground operator and is not liable for the acts or omissions of airlines, hotels, transport companies, or the Agent's failure to advise their clients.
  • Hazardous activities are undertaken at the traveler's own risk.

4. Indemnity

  • The Agent indemnifies Jungle Dwellers against claims, losses, or damages arising from its clients, except where caused by Jungle Dwellers' proven negligence.
